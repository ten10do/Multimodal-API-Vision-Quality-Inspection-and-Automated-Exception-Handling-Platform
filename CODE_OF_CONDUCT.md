# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically honest project environment. Participation should make it easier for people to examine evidence, challenge assumptions, report failures, and improve the system without harassment or retaliation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific when reviewing ideas or work.
- Critique evidence, design, and implementation rather than people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct inaccurate claims promptly.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licenses, and factory-data boundaries.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information.
- Knowingly misrepresenting simulation as production evidence or suppressing material safety limitations.
- Sharing credentials, proprietary data, restricted model artifacts, or confidential site information without authorization.

## Scope and Enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ten10do). Security vulnerabilities must use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and protect reporter privacy.
